# JBeam Forge 0.8.2: the objects library

Ready-made brake parts you can drop into any car, and a way to move models into place.

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.8.2.exe**: the installer. The materials and objects packs are included.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.8.2-portable.exe**: a single exe, with both packs included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.8.2.zip**: the materials pack on its own (284 materials).
- **JBeam-Forge-Objects-0.8.2.zip**: the objects pack on its own (58 objects).

## New

**Objects library**
- A new **Objects** panel (the package icon in the toolbar) lists ready-made parts, each with a rendered preview:
  - 24 brake calipers: Alcon, AP Lockheed, ATE, Brembo, Fiat, TM Performance and more;
  - 34 brake discs, plus a drum.
  - Three digital dashes (Haltech and Link) are waiting on the KN5 importer, which comes next.
- Search by name, or filter by category.
- **Add** brings the object in as its own model with its material already set up: textures, metallic, roughness and normal map. Untextured calipers come in gloss red and discs in cast iron, and you can edit either in the Materials panel.
- Everything has a consistent name: `Brembo 03`, `AP Lockheed 01`, and so on.

**Move models into place**
- Right-click a model at the top of the Scene tree and choose **Placement…**.
- Set position (metres), rotation (degrees) and scale. The viewport updates as you type, every change can be undone, and **Reset** puts it back where it came in.
- This works for any imported model, not just objects.

## Under the hood

- Project files move to format v8 to store each model's placement. Older projects open with everything where it was.
- The materials and objects packs are rebuilt with consistent names and folders throughout.
