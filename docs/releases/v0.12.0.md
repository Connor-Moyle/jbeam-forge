# JBeam Forge 0.12.0: builders, paint studio, vinyls, material brush, sound, props and cameras

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.12.0.exe**: the installer.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.12.0-portable.exe**: a single exe.

The materials and objects packs are no longer included: download them from inside the app (Downloads → Textures / Meshes), all at once or one by one. They're saved in a `JBeam Forge Content` folder beside the app. Projects that used a pack item find it again once it's downloaded.

Projects are now format v19; 0.11 can't open them. Rescan your library (Settings → Library) once, so the suspensions and engines cut from your game list their part options.

## New

**Vehicle scripts** (a new Scripts tab)
- 16 ready-made functions: wipers, power windows (frameless glass drop), folding mirrors, sunroof, convertible roof, power seats, pop-up headlights, welcome and ambient lights, active rear wing, launch control, pops and bangs, shift light, wind and road noise, emergency brake flash, and an Android Auto / CarPlay style head unit on your screen mesh.
- Set them up from a form, pick the meshes they move, choose their keys.
- A Lua editor that checks as you type and explains mistakes, with completions and help for BeamNG's vehicle API.
- A test runner: drive a scenario, press keys, see every value the script writes, and play its animations on the car.
- Save scripts to your library, share them, and download more (Downloads → Scripts).

**Extensions**: add your own commands, script templates and project edits (Settings → Extensions, docs/extensions.md).

**Configurations manager**: every configuration as a card with its picture and figures. Pick the default, reorder, import and save .pc files, compare two side by side, and fill in the vehicle selector details.

**Vehicle selector pictures**: studio pictures like the game's own, for the model and each configuration (size, angle and backdrop in Settings → Export).

**Drive shafts**: the gearbox now reaches axles from other cars. Rear, front or all-wheel drive, with a front/rear split and centre differential for all-wheel drive.

**Downloads and Settings**
- **Downloads** (toolbar, home screen, Ctrl+Shift+D): update the app to the latest GitHub release, or roll back to any older one. Textures and meshes each download from their own repository: everything, only what changed, or single items; any earlier version too.
- **Settings**, reorganised into sections: window size presets (720p to 4K, or remembered), start maximised or full screen, interface scale, render scale, anti-aliasing, frame rate cap, FPS counter, grid, reflections, viewport background, camera field of view, orbit and zoom speed, units (km/h or mph, hp/kW/PS, Nm or lb-ft), autosave, undo steps, recent projects, reopen the last project, export options, content folder, update checks and the download repositories.

**Engine, gearbox and driveline builders**
- **Engine:** drag the dyno curve, stretch the rev range, scale the weight, and change any number the engine has (limiter, inertia, turbo, backfire…).
- **Gearbox:** edit, add or space the ratios, with road speed per gear.
- **Driveline:** each axle's differential: open, LSD, viscous or locked, final drive, preload and locking.
- **More engines:** fit several; each configuration picks one, and players can swap in the parts menu.
- **The game's other parts:** for a suspension's or engine's slots (brakes, steering racks, turbos, ECUs…). Pick which is fitted, and which ship as in-game choices.

**Engine sound**
- Any of the game's engine sounds for intake and exhaust.
- A rev preview with the game's own recordings: play it, drag the revs and throttle, or "Rev it".

**Paints and painting**
- Factory paints and the car's three paint slots, per configuration too. Paint materials preview the way the game mixes them.
- **Paint studio:** brush, fill, patterns (stripes, fades, checks, camo, flake), text and image stamps, eyedropper, mirror both sides, and image round trips.
- **Vinyl editor:** layers of shapes, text and images, livery-editor style: gradients, cut-outs and clipping, groups, mirrored copies, 43 shapes and 10 ready-made designs, and `.jbvinyl` files to reuse on other cars.
- **Material brush:** drag carbon fibre, Kevlar, chrome, Alcantara and more onto the car. The painted triangles export as that real material.
- **Two-sided materials:** a different material on the inside.

**Materials and textures**
- **Channel views:** roughness, metallic, AO, normals and a UV checker.
- **UV tools:** a UV layout preview, and box or planar projection for meshes without good UVs.
- **Ambient occlusion baking** from the car's own shape, into a real texture.
- **Suggestions from the game's own materials** for "Use a BeamNG material".

**Hinges and the sandbox**
- Hinge every door, hood and trunk in one click.
- Sandbox scenarios: **Swing** (open to the stop and shut), **Wrench off** (should tear at the hinges), and **Drop on its suspension** (bottoming out, sag, pitch, bounce).

**Animated parts and cameras**
- The steering wheel, needles (revs, speed, fuel, temperature), pedals and handbrake move in game. Try each in the viewport first.
- Interior cameras: driver, passenger and hood. Look through them in the app.

## Fixed

- Hinged doors swung past their stop: the opening limiter was anchored too close to the hinge line to go taut. It now anchors where it works.
- Install detection with a trailing backslash in BeamNG's ini.

## Please try in-game

All of this changes the export. Please try:
- a door swinging to its stop;
- an alternative brake or turbo;
- an LSD;
- a second engine in a configuration;
- a changed engine sound;
- the steering wheel and needles;
- the driver camera;
- a painted livery, vinyls and a carbon-fibre panel;
- a car with axles and a gearbox from different cars (does it drive?), and all-wheel drive;
- a few scripts: their keys, the head unit screen, the wind noise (docs/scripts.md lists what needs checking).

If anything errors, send me beamng.log.
