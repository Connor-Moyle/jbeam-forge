# JBeam Forge 0.8.4: your own library folders, and BeamNG's suspension parts

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.8.4.exe**: the installer. The materials and objects packs are included.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.8.4-portable.exe**: a single exe, with both packs included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.8.4.zip**: the materials pack on its own (284 materials).
- **JBeam-Forge-Objects-0.8.4.zip**: the objects pack on its own (61 objects).

## New

**Your own library folders**
- **Settings → Library folders:** add any folders of materials (texture sets, MaterialX files) or objects (meshes such as calipers, discs and gauges).
- **Scanning:** JBeam Forge scans them every time it starts, whenever you change the list, or when you press **Scan now**. It uses the same rules as the bundled packs:
  - consistent names;
  - texture roles worked out from file names;
  - EXR converted;
  - packed textures split.
- **Where they show up:** in the Material library and the Objects panel, next to the packs.
- **Speed:** scanning runs in the background and only rescans a folder when something in it changed. The 284-material library takes about 3 seconds.

**Suspension, brake and steering parts from BeamNG**
- **Setup:** once your BeamNG.drive install folder is set, JBeam Forge goes through every stock vehicle and adds its suspension-area parts to the Objects panel (under **BeamNG**).
- **What's included:** 1,030 of them:
  - front and rear suspensions;
  - springs and dampers;
  - brakes and hubs;
  - steering;
  - sway bars;
  - axles and differentials;
  - strut braces;
  - hubcaps.
- **Names:** each part is named after its car, e.g. "ETK 800-Series · Independent Front Suspension".
- **Adding a part:** it comes in as its own model, in the position it has on its car. Move it with **Placement…**. It uses the game's own materials, so your exported mod shows the real ones.
- **Where they stay:** the parts are read from your install and kept on your computer. It takes about 20 seconds the first time, and runs again only after a game update.

## Fixes

- Objects with long names no longer get mixed up with each other in the Objects panel.
- Searching the Objects panel shows the closest matches first.
