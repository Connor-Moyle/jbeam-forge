# Not released yet

Changes on the branch since 0.13.2, collected here for the next release's notes (it becomes `v<version>.md` when it goes out).

## Practice car

- **A real engine under the bonnet**: an inline four laid over like the E30's M10, with its sump, ribbed block, head, cam cover, distributor and plug leads, timing cover, pulleys and belt, fan, alternator, oil filter and bell housing. The intake (runners, plenum, throttle body, hose and air box) and the exhaust manifold (headers, collector and down pipe) are parts of their own. The radiator has its tanks, fan shroud, hoses and expansion tank, and the brake servo and master cylinder sit on the firewall.
- **Clean panel lines**: the bonnet, wings, doors and boot lid come off along straight seams, without the ragged slivers they used to leave behind.
- **Front seats on their own**: each front seat (with its headrest) is exactly the seat, no longer carrying bits of the floor or the rear-view mirror.
- **A boot to look into**: with the boot lid off there's a carpeted floor, sides and a spare-wheel cover.
