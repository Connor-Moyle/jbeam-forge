# JBeam Forge 0.8.3: bring your Assetto Corsa cars over

Import kn5 models, or a whole Assetto Corsa car folder with its skins and data.

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.8.3.exe**: the installer. The materials and objects packs are included.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.8.3-portable.exe**: a single exe, with both packs included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.8.3.zip**: the materials pack on its own (284 materials).
- **JBeam-Forge-Objects-0.8.3.zip**: the objects pack on its own (61 objects).

## New

**kn5 import**
- **Import model** now takes Assetto Corsa `.kn5` files.
  - Meshes, materials and the textures packed inside the file all come in. There's nothing to unpack first.
  - AC material settings become PBR settings, including glow, glass and cut-outs.
- **Car paint and liveries look right.**
  - Kunos-style paint (the colour in `metal_detail`, the livery in the skin texture's alpha) is combined into one texture when you import.
  - That combined texture is what shows in the viewport and what goes into your BeamNG mod.

**Import Assetto Corsa Car** (File menu or command palette)
- **Pick a car folder** (`content/cars/<car>`). JBeam Forge finds:
  - the main model;
  - the skins;
  - the car's data, from the `data` folder or the packed `data.acd`;
  - `ui_car.json`;
  - the Custom Shaders Patch `extension` folder.
- **Before importing**, you see the car's spec sheet. Then:
  - choose a skin;
  - optionally use the car's name, brand and description for your mod.
- **Reference car panel** (the car icon in the toolbar):
  - **Specs:** mass, weight split, wheelbase, track, tyres, engine power and torque peaks, gearing, final drive, diff, brakes and steering.
  - **Files:** every data file, readable.
  - **Skin:** switch skins at any time.
- The data files are saved in the project, so the upcoming suspension, engine and gearbox tools can build from them.

**Objects library**
- Three digital dashes are added: the Haltech iC-7, the Haltech uC-10 and the Link MXG Strada 7. They come with their own materials and textures, and their licences and author credits are kept.

## Under the hood

- Project files move to format v9 to store the reference car. Older projects open as before.
- Tested on a 1.24-million-triangle modded E30 (it imports in under 4 seconds) and on stock Kunos cars.
