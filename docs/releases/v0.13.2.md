# JBeam Forge 0.13.2: an E30 practice car in parts, credits, and save files that always carry over

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.13.2.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.13.2-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.

Projects are now format v20. 0.13.2 opens projects from every earlier version and brings them up to date; the first save keeps the original beside it (`name.jbforge.v19.bak`) so an older app can still open that copy. Earlier versions can't open projects saved by 0.13.2 and say so (update the app).

## New

**The practice car is a BMW E30**, made from "1982 BMW 3 Series E30" by zairiq-zairiq-123-pixar-cars-bfdi on Sketchfab (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1982-bmw-3-series-e30-8d8b44242a52400aae216f7e05b92b36), licensed under CC BY 4.0:
- Smoothed so it's no longer blocky, keeping its sharp edges and panel lines.
- Split into the parts a car comes apart into, so auto-classify sorts it straight away: body, bonnet, boot lid, spoiler, wings, doors, side skirts, bumpers, grille, windscreen, rear and side windows, headlights, tail lights, indicators, mirrors, wheels, tyres, brake calipers, front seats, rear seat, dashboard and steering wheel.
- An engine bay of its own (inner wings with strut towers, firewall, undertray, radiator support) with an engine, radiator and battery, so taking the bonnet off shows something to work on.
- Its textures (lights, tail lights, interior, wheels) come with it.

**Credits** (Help → Credits): every model, texture and piece of software from other people, with their licences and links, plus the authors of credited objects in your library. A mod made from the practice car carries the model's credit in a `credits.txt`.

**Save files always carry over**: projects from every earlier version open in this one with their parts, structure, hinges, animated parts and triggers intact. Real projects saved by earlier versions are kept as test files, and the tests fail if a change to the project format ever comes without an upgrade step. See docs/releasing.md.

**Every release is the next version number**: the release build now refuses a version that isn't newer than the latest release, and never replaces one.
