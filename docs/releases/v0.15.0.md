# JBeam Forge 0.15.0: a tidier window laid out like Blender, and exports that hold up with the game's own parts

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.15.0.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.15.0-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.

Projects stay format v21, so 0.14 and 0.15 open each other's projects. Your panel layout starts fresh once, because the panels have moved.

## The new layout

The window keeps one shape in every workspace: the 3D view in the middle, what you pick from on the left, what you change on the right.

**Properties column.** The ten unlabelled panel buttons that had piled up on the toolbar are gone. Their tools are now tabs of one column on the right, with a strip of icons down its side, the way Blender does it:
- Inspector
- Materials, Paints, Skin studio
- Suspension, Engine & gearbox
- Configurations, Extras
- Objects library, Reference car

Opening one never squeezes another off the screen any more (the Inspector used to end up a few pixels wide). The column widens when a tool needs the room, and each tab stays as you left it while you look at another. Click the tab that's showing to go back to the Inspector.

**The 3D view has its own header.** Edit nodes on the left; mesh, nodes and beams, X-ray and the material channel on the right.

**Toolbar.** File and undo on the left, then **Generate** and **Test**. On the right, **Configurations** and **Export**, then downloads, help and settings. The steps of a mod have their names on them.

**Workspaces**, in the order a car comes together: Parts (was Editing), Modelling, Materials, JBeam, **Suspension** (new), Engine, Moving parts, Triggers, Scripts, Testing. Ctrl+0 opens Suspension.

**Under the view.** The JBeam file and the test results open below the 3D view instead of beside it.

**Your way round** (Settings → Interface):
- Properties column on the right (like Blender) or on the left; the Scene tree takes the other side.
- Words on every toolbar button, for when the icons are new to you.
- Open a project on the Parts workspace, or on whichever workspace was open last.

## Fixed

**Exports with the game's own parts.** Checked against a real BeamNG.drive install (the practice car with an ETK 800 front suspension, inline-6 and 6-speed; and part mods for the ETK 800):
- With a game engine or gearbox fitted, the export refused to go ("has no structure yet"), and Generate built nodes for them that were never used. They're now treated like a fitted suspension: their own nodes and beams go into the mod.
- An engine's mounts on the gearbox's nodes went missing in the game. They now point at the gearbox's nodes.
- Meshes of a fitted engine or suspension that hung on the original car's body or radiator now ride on your car's body.
- Engine mods couldn't be installed: they were written to a folder named after the car's display name ("ETK 800-Series") instead of its folder (etk800).
- Export was greyed out for tyre, wheel, body panel and engine mods until they had parts, which they never need.
- The fitted engine and suspension cards wrap in a narrow column instead of squeezing the name.
- The materials list showed five rows in a tall panel; it fills the panel until a material is picked, and keeps its rows when one is.
- Node editing switched on by the JBeam workspace stayed on in every other workspace.
- Settings → Rescan now refreshes the suspension, engine and panel pickers straight away (missed 0.14.1).
- A download repository that can't be found says it may not be published yet, instead of only "Not found".

## Also

- The tutorial and help centre follow the new layout, with a step for the Properties column.
