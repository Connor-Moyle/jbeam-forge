# JBeam Forge 0.10.0: engines, gearboxes, and a proper gizmo

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.10.0.exe**: the installer. The materials and objects packs are included.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.10.0-portable.exe**: a single exe, with both packs included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.10.0.zip** / **JBeam-Forge-Objects-0.10.0.zip**: the packs on their own.

## New

**Move, rotate and scale in the viewport, like Blender**
- **The gizmo:** select any meshes and press **G** (move), **R** (rotate) or **S** (scale), or use the buttons at the top left of the viewport. Drag the handles, and press the same key or **Esc** to put them away.
- **Whole models move as one:** a fitted suspension, engine or added object stays in one piece. Separate meshes move, turn and resize about the selection's centre.
- **Undo:** each drag is one undo step.

**Engine & gearbox workshop** (the gauge icon)
- **Choosing:** pick an engine by type (Inline-4, Inline-6, V8, Flat-4, Electric…), then brand and car. Each engine shows its power, torque and rev limit with a small dyno chart.
- **Gearboxes** are picked the same way: manual, automatic, dual-clutch, sequential or CVT, with their ratios.
- **Fit:**
  - puts the engine behind the front axle and the gearbox behind the engine;
  - the gizmo fine-tunes where they sit;
  - **Tune** edits their settings (ECU, boost, ratios…).
- **Export:** the engine and gearbox jbeam come over into your mod. The gearbox plugs into the engine's transmission slot.
- **Range:** 157 engines and 194 gearboxes from the current game, read from your own install.

**Your own suspension meshes**
- On a fitted suspension, **Use my selected meshes** shows your own suspension meshes instead of the game's.
- The game's jbeam still does the physics, and your meshes follow its nodes. The game's meshes are hidden and left out of the export.

**Textures on the game's parts**
- Suspensions, engines, gearboxes and the other BeamNG parts now come in with their colour textures instead of plain grey.

**Test Mode**
- **Car mesh** shows your car's meshes bending and moving with the physics, so you can see how the visual mesh deforms.
- **Only selected part** shows just the selected part, to watch how one piece behaves.

## Please try in-game

Engines and gearboxes brought over, like suspensions, need a check in BeamNG. Fit one to a car with generated structure, export it, and send me the log if it doesn't start.
