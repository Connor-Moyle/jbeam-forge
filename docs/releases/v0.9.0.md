# JBeam Forge 0.9.0: the suspension workshop

Put a real BeamNG suspension on your car, axle by axle, with its jbeam brought over and a tuning page.

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.9.0.exe**: the installer. The materials and objects packs are included.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.9.0-portable.exe**: a single exe, with both packs included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.9.0.zip** / **JBeam-Forge-Objects-0.9.0.zip**: the packs on their own.

## New

**Suspension workshop** (the wrench in the toolbar)
- **Axles:**
  - **Set up axles** puts a front and a rear axle where your car's wheels are, or where they would be for a body that size.
  - **Add axle** gives trucks and six-wheelers more.
  - Each axle has its position, track width, and whether it steers.
- **Choose suspension** goes step by step:
  - **Type:** MacPherson strut, double wishbone, multi-link, solid axle, leaf spring, trailing arm, torsion beam, swing axle.
  - **Brand**, with the game's logos.
  - **Car.**
  - **That car's suspensions** for the axle, each with a preview.
- **Fit** brings the complete set, as the game fits it:
  - arms, hubs, struts or coilovers, sway bar, steering and brakes;
  - centred on your car, with the hubs on the axle line;
  - ready to fine-tune with the move arrows (M).
- **The jbeam comes too.** On export the suspension's own jbeam is brought into your mod:
  - renamed so nothing clashes;
  - moved to where you put it;
  - attached to your body's nearest structure;
  - with the game's wheels and tyres still fitting.
- **Tune** shows that suspension's tuning settings (camber, caster, toe, wheel offset, and springs and dampers where it has them). Your values become the defaults, still adjustable in the game.
- There are 145 complete suspensions from the current game. They're read from your own BeamNG.drive install and stay on your computer.
- The game's parts are no longer mixed into the Objects panel.

**Also**
- **Remove model** in the Scene tree's right-click menu on a model.

## Please try in-game

The suspension jbeam transplant is new. Fit a suspension to a car with generated body structure, export, and spawn it. If anything is off, send me BeamNG's log and I'll adjust how it attaches.
