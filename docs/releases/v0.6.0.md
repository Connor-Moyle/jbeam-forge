# JBeam Forge 0.6.0 — physics sandbox

First packaged build. Everything up to and including the physics sandbox (phases 1–6), plus the start of phase 7.

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.6.0.exe**: a normal installer. You pick the install folder, and it adds a Start menu shortcut and an uninstaller.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.6.0-portable.exe**: a single exe with no install. Just run it.

The builds aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will probably say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's in it

**Projects and import**
- Home screen with New mod / Open and a recent projects list with thumbnails.
- `.jbforge` project files, with unlimited undo/redo and an unsaved-changes prompt.
- Import from DAE, FBX, OBJ+MTL, glTF/GLB and STL, including BeamNG `.dds` textures. There's a scale and axis picker, and a "locate folder" step for missing textures.

**Parts**
- Automatic part detection from mesh names: 132 part kinds, with left/right/front/rear positions and variants. On the Hirochi Sunburst it gets about 99% of names right.
- A part hierarchy tree. Doors own their glass, card and handles; drag to re-parent; search.
- New in this build: children are grouped into categories (Doors → Front doors → Front left door, Glass, Lights, Interior, Engine and so on) so big cars stay navigable.
- A search-first assign dialog and bulk assign that spreads positions by bounding box. Custom parts, variants, and part details (name, price, description, construction material).
- Non-destructive mesh splitting: by connected pieces, by face selection (box/lasso/brush/flood fill), or with a cutting plane.

**Node and beam generation**
- A structure is generated for every part. Shells and panels are remeshed to follow the surface, with mirrored left/right and bracing for the main body.
- Node counts and beam values are tuned from official vehicles, and a stability check flags parts likely to explode in game.

**Export**
- Writes a complete unpacked mod straight into your BeamNG mods folder, or a zip. It includes a jbeam per part, slots, flexbodies, DAE meshes, materials, info.json, a default config and a thumbnail.
- The export is checked against a validator before writing.

**Physics sandbox (Test Mode)**
- Runs the generated structure live with stress colours. Drag nodes around with the mouse.
- Test scenarios: settle on jack stands, a 1 m drop, a corner drop, an attachment yank, and pole/wall/offset crashes at a speed you choose.
- Instant pre-checks for loose nodes, disconnected islands and unstable beams.

## Known gaps

- Generated structure still doesn't hug the mesh as tightly as official cars do. More work to come.
- There are no hinges yet, so doors, hood and trunk are bolted shut.
- Materials editor, engine/gearbox/suspension builders and the in-game style part selector are still on the roadmap.
