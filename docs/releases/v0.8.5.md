# JBeam Forge 0.8.5: move anything, mirror it, place objects at the wheels

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.8.5.exe**: the installer. The materials and objects packs are included.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.8.5-portable.exe**: a single exe, with both packs included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.8.5.zip**: the materials pack on its own.
- **JBeam-Forge-Objects-0.8.5.zip**: the objects pack on its own.

## New

**Move, turn and resize any mesh**
- Select one or more meshes (assigned to a part or not). The Inspector's new **Mesh** section has position, rotation and scale.
- Press **M**, or use the **Move** button in the viewport, to drag them with arrows.
- **Mirror to other side** makes a mirrored copy on the other side of the car. It joins the matching part there (a front-left caliper's copy goes to the front-right caliper).
- **Duplicate** makes a copy in place, **Reset** puts a mesh back, and copies can be deleted.
- Everything is undoable and saved with the project, and the export writes the meshes as you placed them.

**Texture scale per part**
- **Texture mapping** in the Mesh section: tiling (U/V scale), offset and rotation for just the selected meshes. Other parts using the same material keep theirs.

**Material settings where you work**
- The Mesh section shows the mesh's material with colour, metallic, roughness and clear coat.
- **All material settings…** opens the full editor on that material, from any layout.

**Objects go where they belong**
- Adding a caliper, disc, drum or hub asks where it goes: **Front left**, **Front right**, **Rear left**, **Rear right**, **All four corners**, or **Not sure**.
- It's placed at your car's own hub, disc or wheel for that corner. If the car has none, it goes where that corner usually is for a car that size.
- It becomes the part for that corner.
- **All four** adds the mirrored and moved copies for you.
- **Not sure** leaves it selected with the move arrows on.

**Hinges (Phase 9b)**
- Parts that open (doors, hood, trunk, tailgate) get a **Hinge** section in the Inspector. **Add hinge** works out the hinge line, the swing direction, the latch and the handles from the part.
- You can then change:
  - how far it opens;
  - which game key opens it;
  - its strength;
  - whether it latches when shut and pops open when released.
- The hinge line, latch and handles can be set from nodes you select.

## Fixes

- **Assetto Corsa cars**: blurred rims, damage glass, the cockpit windscreen reflection, the unbuckled seatbelt and the low-detail cockpit no longer cover the real meshes. They come in hidden and ignored.
- **Assetto Corsa gloss maps** are now used, so paint, chrome and plastic shine as they should.
- **Packed car data (data.acd)** opens even when the car's folder was renamed after it was packed.
