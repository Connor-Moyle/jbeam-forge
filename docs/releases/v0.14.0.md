# JBeam Forge 0.14.0: an Engine workspace with an engine designer, Automation engines, and tutorials for scripts, triggers, moving parts and JBeam

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.14.0.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.14.0-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.

Projects are now format v21. 0.14.0 opens projects from every earlier version and brings them up to date; the first save keeps the original beside it (`name.jbforge.v20.bak`) so an older app can still open that copy. Earlier versions can't open projects saved by 0.14.0 and say so (update the app).

## New

**Engine workspace** (its own tab, Ctrl+9, apart from the suspension)
- The camera glides to the engine (the fitted one, the car's own engine meshes, or where an engine will go), the rest of the car turns see-through, and the camera turns slowly round it until you move it. Both are options, on the page and in Settings.

**Engine designer**: build an engine the way you'd design one.
- Layout and cylinders (inline, V, flat, W, rotary, electric), bore and stroke, block and head material, valvetrain and valves per cylinder, variable valve timing, cam profile, intake and exhaust, turbo / twin turbo / supercharger with boost and turbo size, fuel and fuel system, compression, tune, rev limit, idle and flywheel. Electric motors by power and top speed.
- Ten starting points, the E30's M10 and S14 among them.
- Power, torque, weight, rev limit, reliability, fuel use and a dyno update as you go, with warnings for knock, piston speed and valve float. The numbers land near real engines.
- Your design goes on a game engine, which gives it mounts, shape and sound: the closest one is suggested and fitted in one step, or it goes on the engine you already have. Fine-tune every number afterwards in the builder.

**Import an engine from Automation**: Engine workspace → Import from Automation…, then pick the car you exported from Automation to BeamNG (the zip in BeamNG's mods folder). Its engine is fitted straight away (several: pick one), and its gearbox joins the gearbox list. It works like any other engine from then on: tune it, design on it, and its own jbeam goes into your mod.

**Tutorials, with Watch or Skip**
- **Every script template** (wipers, windows, mirrors, head unit and the rest): offered the first time you add one. It walks the real panel: what it does and needs, every setting, picking its meshes, its keys, the values it writes, a test run, then its code block by block with what every line does, tied to your settings and keys.
- **Triggers, Moving parts and the JBeam workspace**: offered the first time each opens; what each control is and how to set it up.
- Replay any of them with the cap buttons; switch the offers off (or bring them all back) in Settings → General.

**Practice car**
- A real engine under the bonnet: a four-cylinder laid over like the M10, with its pulleys, belt, fan, alternator and plug leads. The intake and exhaust manifold are parts of their own; the radiator has its hoses and expansion tank, and the brake servo sits on the firewall.
- Clean panel lines: the bonnet, wings, doors and boot lid come off along straight seams.
- Each front seat is exactly the seat (no bits of floor or the rear-view mirror).
- A carpeted boot floor with the boot lid off.
