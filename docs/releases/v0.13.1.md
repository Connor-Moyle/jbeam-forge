# JBeam Forge 0.13.1: a Modelling workspace, a new practice car, and fixes from your testing

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.13.1.exe**: the installer. It updates 0.13.0 (or 0.11) in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.13.1-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.

Projects stay format v19: 0.13.0 opens projects saved by 0.13.1, but it leaves out meshes reshaped in the new Modelling workspace (it shows them as the file has them).

## New

**Modelling workspace** (the tab after Editing, Ctrl+8): reshape a mesh the way you would in Blender.
- Pick one mesh and choose **Reshape**. Pick points, edges or faces (1, 2, 3); click to pick, Shift+click to add or remove, A for everything, L for everything joined on.
- Move, turn or resize what is picked with the arrows (G, R, S). **Extrude** (E) pulls faces out, or grows a new face from edges; **Fill** (F) makes a face through 3 or 4 points; **Flip normals** (Alt+N) turns faces that show inside out; **Delete** (X).
- Every change is one undo step, and it is kept in the mod: your model file is never changed. Painted faces, materials and splits stay where they were. **Back to the file's shape** undoes all of it for that mesh.
- **Saved again in Blender?** When a model you reshaped here changes on disk, the app asks which version to keep: the new file (your changes in the app are dropped; Undo brings them back), or yours (the file isn't reloaded for now).

**The old Modelling tab is now called Editing**: the same tools as before (the scene, parts, the move arrows on whole meshes, nodes and beams).

**A new practice car**: the tour's car is now an E30-style saloon (still called the demo car) that comes apart like a real model: doors, bonnet, boot lid, wings, bumpers, glass, lights, indicators, mirrors, seats, dashboard, steering wheel, engine bay parts, wheels, tyres and brakes are each their own piece, with panels that have an inside so they look solid with the next one off.

**Suspension check** (the Suspension panel, and the Testing tab): drop the car onto its wheels, or push the body down by hand, and see each tyre's gap to the body at full bump and at rest, and the ground clearance. A quick visual check before the game.

**Place game parts right after fitting them**: a fitted engine, gearbox or suspension is picked up with the move arrows as soon as it's in, and has a Move button on its card; its physics moves with it.

**Build your own engine or gearbox** is now said plainly on the engine and gearbox cards.

**Moving parts: move the part itself**: previews swing the real door, bonnet or boot (put back afterwards) instead of a see-through copy. The choice is remembered.

**Skin studio in three steps**: choose the panels, lay them out, then save the template or make a skin from a painted one, one clear button per step. Parts from the game (fitted engines and suspensions, a panel mod's guide, parts using BeamNG's own materials) are never listed or laid out.

**Structure closer to the car's shape**: cross-car bracing only at the floor, roof and ends (no longer through the cabin), and a denser body, so fewer beams float outside the panels.

## Fixed

- **Export crash** "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'toFixed')": models with missing normals, colours or out-of-range triangles now export (the odd triangle is skipped and logged).
- **Dropdowns inside windows** (Settings resolution, frame rate and every other list) opened behind the window.
- **Materials panel**: the editor has the whole right column, and the preview folds away on short screens.
- **Copy error report** gives a short report (what happened, where, the last few problems) instead of a huge dump.
- **Test Mode**: the car shows from the start, and the mesh toggle (Alt+1) works there too.
- **Scripts**: survive a vehicle reset in the game (they failed after pressing R), keys are set by pressing them, and sliders work when the panel is narrow.
- **Slowing down after a while**: repeated errors can no longer flood the log, and a performance line goes into the log every 2 minutes so a report shows what was happening.
- **Tour**: step instructions look like instructions, not links, and say where to click. Closing the practice car with the tour open no longer hides the save prompt, and Test Mode stops when a mod closes.
