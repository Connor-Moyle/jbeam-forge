# JBeam Forge 0.8.1 — the materials pack

284 ready-made materials now come built into the app, with their textures.

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.8.1.exe**: the installer (the materials pack is included).
- **JBeam-Forge-0.8.1-portable.exe**: a single exe, pack included.
- **JBeam-Forge-Materials-0.8.1.zip**: the materials pack on its own, for sharing or for adding to another copy (Material library → Import).

The downloads are bigger than before (about 460 MB) because the pack's textures come with them.

## New

**Materials pack, built in**
- Open the Material library (the book icon in the Materials panel). **Materials pack** is the first tab, with a thumbnail for every material, search and a category filter.
- **What's in it:**
  - 89 textured materials: carbon fiber, forged carbon, fiberglass, alcantara, leathers, fabrics, carpets, aluminium, brass, copper, gold, chrome, stainless, metal plates, grilles, brake discs, radiators, plastics, rubber and wood.
  - Plus 195 flags.
- **Apply** puts one on the selected meshes, and **+** adds it to the project. From there every setting is editable in the Materials panel: colour, metallic, roughness, opacity, normal strength, clear coat, glow, every texture slot, transparency, alpha cut-out, and more.
- Grilles are set up as see-through cut-outs out of the box.
- Every material exports to BeamNG with its textures copied into your mod.

**Better looking metals**
- The viewport now has studio reflections, so chrome, gold, brushed aluminium and clear-coated paint look like themselves instead of flat grey.

**Import material packs**
- The library's import button takes a whole pack `.zip` as well as single `.jbmat` files. Re-importing only adds materials you don't have yet.

## Under the hood

- Hinge groundwork for the next phase: doors can be given a real hinge, a limiter, a latch and handles, exported the way the stock cars do it. The tools to set these up in the app come next.
