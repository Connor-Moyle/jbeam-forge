# JBeam Forge 0.16.0 test build 3: cars that spawn whole and drive, checks in the game, and more warnings before you export

> **Test build.** 0.16.0 for testing before it goes out to everyone. It shows in the app only with Settings → Downloads → "Include pre-release (test) versions". Please report what breaks: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Connor-Moyle/jbeam-forge/issues).

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.16.0-test.3.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.16.0-test.3-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.16.0-test.3_amd64.deb**: Linux (Ubuntu, Mint, Pop!_OS…): `sudo apt install ./jbeam-forge_0.16.0-test.3_amd64.deb`.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.16.0-test.3.AppImage**: Linux, any distribution: make it executable and run it. The Linux version has not been tried on a real Linux PC with BeamNG yet.
- **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game, if you'd rather drop it in your mods folder by hand. The app installs and updates it for you (Settings → BeamNG.drive).

Projects are now format v25. The game's parts are read again once after updating (a few minutes), because what is kept about each of them has changed.

## Cars that spawn whole

Tested by building one body with the front suspension, rear suspension, engine and gearbox of 24 of the game's cars in turn and spawning each in BeamNG.drive 0.39: all 24 spawn steady and drive, and 21 spawn with no broken beam.

- Generated structure is built to the game's own node counts and stiffness, with left and right parts mirrored and thin panels stiffened from behind.
- Structure keeps out of the wheels. A wing wrapped in a hull was closed across its arch, and the tyre pushed it out of shape or broke it.
- Hinges, catches and a borrowed set's mounting points are tied to the body until they are held in every direction. A bonnet catch that wasn't never latched.
- A borrowed suspension's mounting points are read from its car as the game ships it, not from a stretched or long-bed frame that names the same points metres further back.
- Parts of the game's that fit several slots (117 of them) now fit their slots in your mod.
- A gearbox that brings its own transfer case keeps it, so four-wheel-drive pickups and vans drive.
- Lower arms no longer snap at spawn on five of the game's front suspensions.
- A fitted engine sits where your car's own engine is, with the gearbox on its block, and an engine with nitrous keeps its own fuel tanks.

## Checks in the game

JBeam Forge in the game (F10) has a **Check** button: it unlatches and shuts every door and lid, drives the car round a skidpad, and runs it into a bollard at 50 km/h, then says pass or fail for each. The Test panel in the desktop app shows the last results for the car.

## Before you export

Export now warns about things that go wrong in the game rather than leaving you to find them at spawn:

- a suspension under a car much heavier or lighter than the one it came from
- tyres that sit inside your car's own structure
- a fitted engine bigger than your car's own engine model
- more nodes, beams, triangles or meshes than the heaviest of the game's own cars

## Also new

- Scripts: traction control, stability control and drive modes, which share the throttle so a car can have all three.
- Sandbox crash tests from behind, sideways into a pole, on the roof, and a wall crash at five speeds in a row.
- Suspension tab: work a fitted suspension through its travel and read off camber and toe (works on about four in ten of the game's suspensions so far, mostly rear ones).
- Command palette: start the car's structure as a hatchback, coupé, saloon, pickup, truck or trailer.
- File → Export Model: FBX, glTF, OBJ, DAE, STL and PLY, with the fitted parts where they stand.

## Known problems

- Bonnets and boot lids unlatch but don't lift on their own yet. Doors are fine.
- Parts from very different vehicles on one car (a dump truck's axle under a hatchback) can still come apart at spawn.
- An engine much bigger than the engine bay breaks its own mounts. Export warns; it doesn't fix it.
- Trailers are not supported beyond a tow hitch.
